Hi, I am Bang Du, an undergraduate student in Physics (minor in Electrical Engineering) at Southern University of Science and Technology (SUSTech). In 2026 I was a visiting undergraduate researcher at the Mechanical Systems Control Lab, BAIR, UC Berkeley, working with Prof. Masayoshi Tomizuka. Before that, I worked on aerial navigation at the STAR Lab, SUSTech, with Prof. Boyu Zhou.
