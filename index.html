About Me

Hi! I am Bang Du, an undergraduate student in Physics (minor in Electrical Engineering) at Southern University of Science and Technology (SUSTech). I am currently a visiting undergraduate researcher at the Mechanical Systems Control Lab, BAIR, UC Berkeley. Before that, I worked on aerial navigation at the STAR Lab, SUSTech.

My long-term research goal is to improve the intelligence and physical interaction capabilities of embodied robots. Toward this goal, my current research focuses on:

  • World action models and world models.
  • Data-centric robot learning.

I have been fortunate to be mentored by Prof. Masayoshi Tomizuka and Prof. Boyu Zhou.

I am currently seeking PhD opportunities for Fall 2027.
